Privacy Policy · Last updated: September 17, 2026

  • Developer: Valentin Trantescu

This policy explains what data the OrtodoxRO app processes, for what purpose, and what control you have over it.

2. General Principles

  • OrtodoxRO does not use cross-app or cross-website tracking.
  • OrtodoxRO does not use any tracking domains.
  • Data is used primarily for app functionality.

3. Data Processed

3.1 Data you enter in the app

The app may store locally on your device:

  • usage preferences (language, notification options, calendar filters, export settings);
  • friends list (name, aliases, phone number, birthday and greeting preferences);
  • local state required for operation (e.g., widget settings and Premium status in App Group).

3.2 Contact data (optional)

If you use the auto-fill from Contacts feature, the app opens the iOS system contact picker. The picker runs outside the app, so OrtodoxRO is never granted access to your address book and cannot read your contacts: it receives only the single contact you tap.

From the contact you choose, the name, first phone number and birthday are copied into the friend profile, for greetings, name day matching and birthday reminders. Only the day and month of the birthday are stored — the year of birth is not.

3.3 Greeting message composition

When you send a greeting, the app opens the native SMS composer or Share Sheet with the phone number and message text, without transmitting this data to external servers. The actual sending is done only through your explicit action.

3.4 iOS Calendar data (optional)

If you enable synchronization, the app creates/updates events in a dedicated OrtodoxRO calendar within the iOS Calendar app.

3.5 Subscription data

For Premium, subscription validation and payment flow are managed through the App Store / StoreKit (Apple).

3.6 Apple Watch app

The liturgical calendar is computed locally on the watch, from data bundled with the app. From your iPhone, the watch receives only two pieces of information, over WatchConnectivity: your Premium subscription status and the language selected in the app.

Your friends list, birthdays and phone numbers are not sent to the watch. The transfer happens directly between your own devices, through Apple's mechanisms, with no intermediate servers.

4. iOS Permissions Used

The app may request the following permissions, depending on the features you activate:

  • Calendar: to export/sync liturgical events to iOS Calendar.
  • Notifications: for liturgical reminders, name day reminders and friends' birthday reminders.

You can revoke these permissions at any time from iOS Settings.

No permission is required for Contacts: the app uses the system contact picker, which hands it only the contact you select, without granting access to the rest of your address book.

5. Legal Basis and Purposes

Data is processed for:

  • providing the app's core functionality;
  • personalizing the experience (settings, filters, language);
  • sending notifications only if you have enabled this feature;
  • managing Premium entitlements through Apple's mechanisms.

6. Data Sharing

OrtodoxRO does not sell personal data.

Data may be involved in the following integrations required for operation:

  • Apple Calendar (when exporting, through the permission you granted);
  • Apple Notifications (for notification delivery);
  • App Store / StoreKit (for subscription and entitlement validation).

7. Storage and Retention

  • Most usage data is stored locally on your device.
  • Data remains stored until you modify/delete it from the app or uninstall the app.
  • Events already exported to iOS Calendar may remain there until you explicitly delete them from Calendar (depending on your configuration).

8. Background Refresh

The app may use iOS Background App Refresh to periodically re-synchronize notifications (approximately every 24 hours). This operation is entirely local — no data is transmitted to external servers.

9. Local Diagnostics

OrtodoxRO maintains a small number of local counters (for example, how many greetings have been sent) to improve the user experience. This data is stored in UserDefaults on the device and is not transmitted outside the app.

10. Security

The app uses the security mechanisms provided by the iOS platform and the Apple frameworks it relies on. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access, rectification, or deletion of personal data.

Practical options within the app:

  • you can edit/delete friend data directly from the dedicated section;
  • you can disable notifications and revoke permissions from iOS Settings;
  • you can disable Calendar synchronization.

For additional requests, contact the developer at vt_apps@yahoo.com.

12. Children

OrtodoxRO is not specifically designed for intentional collection of personal data from children under the minimum applicable age in your jurisdiction.

13. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect product, technical, or legal changes. The current version will have the last update date mentioned in the header.
